THE INFINITE SHIFT

FOUR LEVELS · ESCALATING DREAD

WARNING: flashing imagery, loud audio, jump-scares, blood and gore, an always-watching entity, and themes of pursuit and identity erasure. Headphones strongly recommended. Each level descends deeper and faster than the last.

MOVE WASD / ARROWS  ·  LOOK MOUSE  ·  SPRINT SHIFT  ·  FLASHLIGHT F  ·  HIDE E  ·  F12 / G GODMODE

Collect every soul-file on a floor (the amber arrow leads you), then the arrow turns green toward the exit. Descend through Level 0 → 1 → 2 → 3; each is bigger, faster, and darker. A red glow on the screen edge warns which way the Auditor is. The Auditor is slower than your walk — you can always escape if you keep moving. A pale figure (the Hush) may appear down a corridor; do not stare at it. Hide in lockers; it is always safe there. GODMODE (F12 or G): rainbow everything, no scares, infinite stamina and light, the Auditor wanders harmlessly — toggle it any time.
